# Quantitative clay-break evidence

Only quantities actually supported by recovered sources are recorded here. Missing values are not reconstructed.

Evidence labels used:

- **MEASURED** — source reports direct measurement/observation.
- **SOURCE_DERIVED** — arithmetic or structured extraction directly from source data.
- **AUTHOR_CLAIM** — author's stated interpretation/association.
- **MODEL_DERIVED** — output/relationship from a model described by a source.
- **INFERRED** — inference made in this investigation.
- **ANECDOTAL** — informal report.
- **UNKNOWN** — provenance or basis insufficient.

## 2. Roster 2018 — stationary sporting-clays matrix

Source and URL as above.

Protocol reported for the sidebar results:

- standard White Flyer targets;
- stationary/non-spinning;
- edge-on or dome-on;
- 30, 40 and 50 yd;
- American shot sizes: No. 7½ = 0.095 in, No. 8 = 0.090 in, No. 8½ = 0.085 in;
- 12-gauge, 1 oz lead target loads;
- Beretta 686, 30 in barrel;
- MobilChoke IC/Modified/Full tested;
- result counted as broken when at least one visible chip was seen coming off;
- sidebar cells are number broken out of a **25-target sample** for the condition represented.

## 4. Pautler — quantitative experiment described, raw dataset absent

Source: James Anthony Pautler, *Analysis of Skeet Target Breakage*, US20210270567A1 / US12264896B2 / WO2020006095A1.

Recovered quantitative structure:

- probabilities of 0, 1, 2, and 3-or-more pellet hits are treated separately in the disclosed method;
- the patent states that pellets were fired at clay targets at controlled velocities;
- pellet number was varied;
- break/no-break was visually observed;
- break distribution was related to pellet kinetic energy.

**Label:** the existence/design of the laboratory experiment is **AUTHOR_CLAIM in a primary patent disclosure**; the probability construction is **MODEL_DERIVED**.

**Not recovered:** raw velocity levels, pellet size/mass, trial counts, individual break/fail observations, target type, target state, exact impact location, uncertainty, fitted coefficients/parameters. Therefore no numerical break curve is entered here.

## 6. Quantitative evidence deliberately not promoted

The following are **not** supplied as calibrated BangLab relationships:

- universal pellet-energy threshold;
- universal pellet-count threshold;
- probability of scoring break from one pellet;
- probability of scoring break from N pellets;
- impact-location multiplier;
- angle multiplier;
- spin coefficient;
- temperature/moisture/age correction;
- target-manufacturer transfer factor.

No recovered dataset currently supports those quantities with sufficient scope and provenance.
