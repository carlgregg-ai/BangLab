## S01 — Tom Roster, “The Best Loads for Skeet and Sporting Clays”

- **Author:** Tom Roster
- **Date:** April 2018
- **Publication:** Clay Target Nation
- **URL:** https://nssa-nsca.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/CTN0418_FE_TestingLoads.pdf
- **Type:** practitioner technical article / controlled shooting tests
- **Access:** public PDF
- **Classification:** practitioner; direct experimental observations
- **Inspection:** **FULL TEXT INSPECTED** (2-page article)
- **Contains:** 100-target recovered-struck skeet experiment for No. 9; repeat with No. 8; stationary sporting-clays matrix for No. 7½/8/8½ at 30/40/50 yd in edge/dome attitudes; test-method detail; pattern-density observations.
- **Evidential status:** direct evidence that struck targets can fail to produce scoring-visible chips; quantitative matrix usable as test-specific evidence. Author interpretations about energy, hardness and spin remain author claims unless independently supported.
- **Binary stored here:** no.

## S03 — James Anthony Pautler, “Analysis of Skeet Target Breakage”

- **Inventor:** James Anthony Pautler
- **Identifiers:** US20210270567A1; US12264896B2; WO2020006095A1; EP3814715A1
- **Priority:** 2018-06-30; PCT filed 2019-06-26; US publication 2021-09-02; US grant 2025-04-01
- **URLs:** https://patents.google.com/patent/US20210270567A1/en ; https://patents.justia.com/patent/12264896
- **Type:** patent family
- **Access:** public patent text
- **Classification:** primary patent disclosure
- **Inspection:** **FULL RELEVANT PATENT TEXT INSPECTED**
- **Contains:** statement of a laboratory experiment firing pellets at clay targets at controlled velocities, varying pellet number and visually observing break/no-break; probability construction using measured break probability and kinetic energy.
- **Evidential status:** establishes that an empirical calibration experiment was claimed/performed and describes its conceptual variables. Raw observations, sample sizes and sufficient calibration metadata were not recovered.
