# Claims control

## v0.1 claim ceiling
BangLab v0.1 outputs are model predictions of geometric pellet contacts with a declared clay silhouette, for the DEP 99/953 reference load at 20–50 m, under declared assumptions, with variant bands. They are not predictions of breakage, scoring, Carl's cartridge, a DTL trajectory, or any actual fired cloud.

## Never claim
- geometric contact is breakage or scoring probability;
- `P(N>=1)` is “hit probability”;
- an oracle/animation realisation is an actual fired cloud;
- the Gaussian lateral fit is validated by the two points used to calibrate it;
- radial agreement validates longitudinal shot-string structure;
- DEP represents Carl's cartridge unless explicitly labelled `USER_LATERAL` + `DEP_PROXY`;
- a computed 3-D string is experimentally validated.

No epistemic status may be promoted without explicit evidence and a versioned decision.
